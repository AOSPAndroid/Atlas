Download current users and desks (.xlsx)

Columns: Desk ID, Name, Team, Account, Status. Use a login, email or BRID in Account to identify someone reliably, including after a name change. New names can be seated without an account; this does not create a directory account.

For a vacancy, clear Name and Account and set Status to vacant. For an assignment, set Status to assigned. A recognised move clears the person's old desk automatically. Positions and equipment stay in place.

Leave this unchecked for a partial update. Omitted people stay seated unless a recognised move relocates them. Removing someone from seating does not delete their directory account.
